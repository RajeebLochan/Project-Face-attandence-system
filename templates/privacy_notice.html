Face recognition privacy notice

Version {{ privacy_notice_version }}. This is a template: the institution must complete the bracketed parts before collecting photos.

What we collect

Your name, e-mail address, registration number, branch, year and batch; one enrolment photo of your face and a numerical face template made from it; your check-in and check-out times. Camera frames at the kiosk are analysed and discarded, not stored.

Why

To record attendance. A staff member confirms every match before anything is recorded.

Your choice

Face recognition is optional. Without consent, or after withdrawing it, staff record your attendance manually. Withdrawing consent deletes your photo and face template; attendance history is kept.

E-mails

If you or your parent or guardian opt in and confirm the address, the institution may e-mail when attendance is recorded. These e-mails never include photos.
