What we collect

Your name, e-mail address, registration number, branch, year, one enrolment photo of your face, and a numerical face template derived from it. When you use the attendance kiosk we record your check-in and check-out times. Camera frames are analysed and immediately discarded; they are not stored.

Why

Only to record attendance. A staff member confirms every recognition before attendance is recorded.

Your choice

Face recognition is optional. If you do not consent, or later withdraw consent, staff record your attendance manually. Withdrawing consent deletes your photo and face template; your attendance history is kept as an institutional record.

Who can see it

Only authorised attendance staff. Photos are never published or shared.
