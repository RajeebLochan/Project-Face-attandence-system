How statuses are decided
  • Present and late need a schedule for the student’s batch. Late means checked in after the expected start plus the grace period.
  • Checked in means attendance was recorded but no schedule applies, so lateness is not judged.
  • Absent is only used for past working days with a schedule, no check-in, no leave and no holiday.
  • Not yet recorded covers today, future days and days with no schedule. Absence is never inferred without a schedule.
  • Schedules, rosters and holidays are effective-dated, so changing them today does not rewrite earlier days.
